Datasets and resources

  • Radicalisation indicators Manosphere forum data labelled for longevity, hostility and centrality, plus a white supremacist forum lexicon.
  • LISTN Manosphere lexicons.
  • WikiTactics Wikipedia Talk page disagreements annotated according to their dispute tactics.
  • WikiEvolve Wikipedia article snapshots for promotional tone detection.
  • WikiDisputes A dataset of disagreements on Wikipedia Talk pages.

Collaborative resources

  • BRIGHTER Emotion-annotated datasets for 28 languages.
  • SemEval-2026 Task 7 Everyday knowledge across diverse languages and cultures.
  • SemEval-2025 Task 11 Text-based emotion detection.
  • SemRel2024 Semantic textual relatedness datasets for 13 languages, used in SemEval-2024 Task 1.
  • RAEmoLLM Code for retrieval-augmented cross-domain misinformation detection.
